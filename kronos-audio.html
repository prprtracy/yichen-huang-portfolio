02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

From existing foundation to ongoing product ownership.

I joined after an initial version of the site had been started by another developer. My work has been extending and maintaining that foundation—not claiming sole authorship of the original build.

WEBSITE

Product and content experience

Develop and refine the production site’s product pages, news, press, distributor information, and customer-facing details as the catalogue evolves.

CONTENT SYSTEM

Structured, maintainable updates

Work with the site’s JSON-driven content modules so product facts and editorial updates can be managed consistently across pages.

TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION

Instructions grounded in the hardware

Rebuild assembly and packing instructions across the product line using hands-on product knowledge: sequences, parts, measurements, diagrams, and safety notes.

PRODUCTION

Real releases, real accountability

Carry approved content and site improvements through to the live website, checking that links, product specifications, and downloadable resources match the current product.
