DISCORD OPERATIONS & TIME LEDGER BOT

Operational bookkeeping,
inside the community.

A Discord community was managing purchased playtime, completed sessions, and staff settlement manually. I built a Discord-native ledger that keeps balances consistent, restricts administrative actions, records every change, and automates the community events surrounding the workflow.

DEPLOYMENT3 serversProduction communities
REACH~1,000 membersAcross all servers
INTERFACE22 commandsNative Discord UX
TENANCYGuild-scopedServer data isolation
01 / ARCHITECTURE

Modular, asynchronous,
guild-aware.

The bot uses discord.py Cogs to separate ledger commands, per-server configuration, and community events. SQLite persistence and image rendering sit behind those interaction layers.

02 / LEDGER WORKFLOW

Three balances,
one source of truth.

Members can query their own remaining playtime through a private Discord response; staff manage the shared ledger through permission-gated commands.

04 / ENGINEERING

Built for the cases
that happen after launch.

⌁

Guild-level isolation

Every companion, account binding, setting, and history query is scoped by guild_id.

♙

Role-based control

Staff operations use a configurable Discord role with owner and server-admin safeguards.

↺

Safe balance rules

Reports are capped at remaining time; settlements cannot exceed the pending balance.

≡

Complete audit trail

Add, report, settle, edit, bind, and delete operations retain operator and timestamp context.

⇣

Excel export

Authorized staff can export a formatted XLSX snapshot directly through a private response.

⚡

Non-blocking logs

Operational logs run as background tasks with cache lookup, timeout, and permission handling.
